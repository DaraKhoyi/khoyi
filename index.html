What PrismOS does

Built for a working real estate agent’s day.

Inbox that knows your clients

Your email sits next to the client, lead or deal it is about. New enquiries are spotted and brought to you, and replies you approve go out from your own address.

Calendar and bookings

Appointments and showings stay in step with your calendar in both directions, and clients can book a time with you from your own booking link.

Contacts and pipeline

Every person’s emails, calls, texts, notes and transactions in one record, with reminders so nobody you care about slips through.

AI that waits for your yes

Summaries, follow-up drafts and a daily plan. Nothing is sent unless you send it, approve it or schedule it yourself.

Your Google data, in plain words

Connecting Google is optional. Here is exactly what it does.

Gmail
PrismOS reads your mail so each message appears next to the right client or lead, and sends, archives, labels, snoozes or moves mail to trash only when you do.
Google Calendar events
PrismOS shows your events and keeps appointments, showings and booking-link meetings in two-way sync with your calendar.
Google Contacts, read-only
PrismOS imports your saved contacts so calls, texts and email match the right person. It never changes anything in Google Contacts.
Basic profile
Your Google name and email address, so PrismOS can show which account is connected.
  • Your Google data is shown only to you. Your broker, team leaders and teammates cannot read your mail, including during support sessions.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and remove PrismOS’s access from your Google Account.
  • PrismOS’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
