Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PrismOS collects, uses, stores, protects and shares information, including information we receive from Google when you connect a Google account. PrismOS is a private software platform used by the licensed real estate agents, team leaders and staff of our brokerage.

1. Who we are

PrismOS is operated by Teamkar Realty, Inc. d/b/a Realty ONE Group Advantage, a Florida real estate brokerage based in Lutz, Florida (“we,” “us” or “our”). We are responsible for the information processed through PrismOS. You can reach us at dara@brokerdara.com.

2. Information we collect

Account information

Your name, email address, role, team and the password you set (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).

Information you add

Contacts, leads, transactions, tasks, notes, documents, voice notes and recordings, and other content you enter or upload.

Phone activity from Quo

If your brokerage phone line runs on Quo (formerly OpenPhone), PrismOS receives the calls, text messages, voicemails and call recordings for that line, along with the phone numbers and contact names involved, so they appear on the right contact and lead.

Technical information

Basic sign-in and error logs, device and browser type, and, if you turn them on, push notification subscription details for your browser or phone.

Other connected services

If you choose to connect Dropbox or iCloud, PrismOS accesses only the files or calendars needed for the features you turn on.

4. How we use information

We use information, including Google user data, only to provide and improve the features of PrismOS that you use, specifically to:

  • show your email, calendar, contacts, calls and texts in one place, connected to the right people and deals;
  • alert you, and only you, to new leads and messages that need your attention;
  • prepare summaries, briefings, task suggestions and draft replies for you to review;
  • send emails or texts that you write, approve or schedule;
  • keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix problems and meet legal obligations.

PrismOS does not send email from your Gmail account on its own. Messages go out only when you send them, approve a draft, or set up a scheduled send.

5. AI features

Some PrismOS features use artificial intelligence services to read content and produce a result for you, such as sorting your inbox, summarizing a thread or a call, drafting a reply, or finding the right document. To do this, the relevant content (which can include Google user data such as an email or calendar event) is sent securely to the AI provider handling that feature, processed to return a result, and is not used by us for any other purpose.

  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models, and our agreements and settings with AI providers do not permit them to use it for that purpose.
  • Before any content is sent to an AI provider, PrismOS automatically removes Social Security numbers, tax IDs, card numbers and bank account numbers that it detects.
  • AI output can be wrong. Drafts are suggestions for you to review, edit and approve before anything is sent.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use or share Google user data for advertising, including personalized, retargeted or interest-based advertising. We do not share Google user data with data brokers or information resellers, and we do not use it to determine credit-worthiness or for lending purposes.

7. Human access to Google user data

No person at our brokerage reads your Google user data (such as the content of your emails) unless (a) you give your permission, for example by asking for help with a specific message; (b) it is necessary for security purposes, such as investigating abuse or a security incident; (c) it is necessary to comply with applicable law; or (d) the data has been aggregated and anonymized and is used for our internal operations.

8. Google API Services: Limited Use disclosure

PrismOS’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, we use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described in this policy; we transfer it to others only as necessary to provide those features, for security purposes, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition; we do not use it for advertising; we do not allow humans to read it except as described in section 7; and we do not use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

9. How we store and protect information

  • All information is stored with Supabase in the United States, in a database and file storage that are encrypted at rest (AES-256).
  • All connections to PrismOS, to Google and to our providers use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Database row-level security limits each person’s synced email, calendar events, Google contacts, calls and texts to that person’s own account. The only exception is the audited support feature described in section 6.
  • Google access tokens are kept only on our servers, are never shown in the app, and are used only by PrismOS server functions to sync the services you connected.
  • Administrative access to our systems is limited to a small number of authorized people, and secret keys are kept in protected server storage.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users of a security breach as required by law.

10. Retention

We keep information for as long as your PrismOS account is active or as needed to provide the service. Real estate brokerages are also required to keep certain transaction records; we keep those for the period the law requires. Some audio recordings are deleted automatically after transcription while the transcript is kept. When you request deletion, we delete or anonymize your information as described below, except where we must keep specific records by law.

11. Your choices: revoking access and deleting data

Disconnect or revoke Google access

  • In PrismOS: go to Settings → App Setup → Connected Google Accounts and tap Disconnect. PrismOS immediately stops syncing that account.
  • At Google: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose PrismOS and select Remove access. This revokes PrismOS’s access to your Google account entirely.

Delete your data

Disconnecting stops new data from flowing in, but does not by itself erase what was already synced. To have your Google user data, or your entire PrismOS account and its data, deleted, email dara@brokerdara.com from the address on your account. We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days, including removing stored Google tokens and the emails, events and contacts synced from Google. Copies in encrypted backups are overwritten on our provider’s regular backup cycle.

You can also ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, at the same address.

12. Children

PrismOS is a business tool for adult real estate professionals. It is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the applicable age), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make a material change, especially to how we use Google user data, we will update the date above and notify users in PrismOS or by email before the change takes effect, and ask for your consent where required.
